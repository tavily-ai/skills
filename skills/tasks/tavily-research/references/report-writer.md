# Writing the report

You turn the research notes into one report that answers the question. You do
not search or read new pages. A claim that is not in the notes, with a URL,
does not go in the report.

## Read first

Read every notes file in the folder you were given. When the assignment names
an earlier report and its notes, read those too. Where they disagree with the
new notes, the new notes win. The report has to stand on its own. The reader
is not sent back to the earlier file.

Paths may contain spaces. Quote them in shell commands.

## Shape

```markdown
# {Title: about six words, with a verb, stating the finding}

{One paragraph that answers the question, then the stakes and the context the reader needs. Someone who stops here should still know the answer.}

## {Signpost heading that states a specific finding}
{Prose. **Bold** the figures and names a reader would scan for. Cite a checkable claim as ([Source](URL)).}

## {Next finding}
{Same form.}

## Conclusion
{One or two paragraphs on what the findings imply. Nothing restated from above.}
```

Use one section for each part of the question, and give each named entity,
region or mechanism its own subsection with its figures.

## Headings

Sentence case. A reader who reads only the headings should get the argument.
Put the specific result in the heading.

| Writes the finding | Names the topic |
|---|---|
| Migratory geese forage 370 extra hours a year | Effects of climate on geese |
| Usage pricing costs more past 50 seats | Pricing |

## Prose

Use a table when several items share the same fields. Each section adds a step
in the argument.

Cite the claims a reader would want to check: figures, dates, and attributed
positions. Use `([Source](URL))` inline. Skip a finding whose note has no URL.

When the notes disagree, say so in the paragraph and say which source you take
and why. State what nobody confirmed. Take a position the evidence supports,
and say what is still open without filling it with "may" or "possibly".

The conclusion is the implication. It is not a summary of the sections.
